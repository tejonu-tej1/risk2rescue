How Risk2Rescue Works

From raw hazard data to relocation decisions — here's the pipeline.

Continuous Operational Pipeline

The 4-Stage Intelligence Engine

Automated ingestion, AI polygon classification, vulnerability carrying capacity analysis, and authoritative relocation guidance.

📡 01
Stage 01

Ingest Live Hazard Data

Pulls from USGS, IMD, Open-Meteo, and seismic/weather feeds in real time to capture active environmental threats.

🧠 02
Stage 02

AI Red Zone Detection

The AI engine classifies hazard polygons into Red/Orange/Yellow/Green tiers based on live intensity and historical disaster data.

⚖️ 03
Stage 03

Vulnerability & Capacity Scoring

Computes a Vulnerability Priority Index per habitation and checks safe-site capacity against at-risk population.

🚨 04
Stage 04

Actionable Relocation Guidance

Ranks habitations for immediate/short-term/medium-term relocation and surfaces it to SDMA/NDRF authorities.

Full-Stack Capabilities

Platform Features

Engineered for mission-critical disaster resilience, field evacuation coordination, and automated triage.

🗺️

Live Multi-Hazard Red Zone Mapping

Dynamic geospatial polygon rendering for cyclone paths, coastal storm surges, river flooding, and seismic hazard zones.

⛺

Carrying Capacity Assessment

Calculates safe-zone absorption thresholds to prevent emergency relief camp overcrowding during mass evacuations.

📊

AI Vulnerability Priority Index

Multi-criteria scoring model weighing infrastructure fragility, elevation, elderly demographics, and hazard proximity.

🆘

Citizen SOS Beacon

One-touch offline-capable emergency beacon transmitting precise GPS telemetry and family triage requirements.

🌩️

Real-Time Weather/Seismic Feeds

Direct live streams from USGS GeoJSON, IMD CAP alerts, and Open-Meteo microclimate telemetry updated continuously.

🏛️

Authority Command Center & Simulator

Interactive incident command cockpit featuring what-if disaster simulation scenarios and automated resource allocation.

📋

Citizen Report Queue

Live crowdsourced damage, waterlogging, and road obstruction reports validated with automated geofencing.

🧭

Evacuation Route Planning

Algorithmic turn-by-turn routing via OpenStreetMap/OSRM that dynamically circumvents active Red Zone hazard perimeters.
